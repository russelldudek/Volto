VOLTO CONSULTING · 90-DAY ENTRY PLAN

Earn the right to scale.

A practical launch plan for ChatGPT power-user enablement, workflow automation, governance, and adoption.

0-30

Read the work and establish the baseline

  • Confirm whether scope is internal, client-facing, or hybrid; identify sponsors, workspace admins, security/privacy partners, functional owners, power users, and frontline adopters.
  • Baseline activation, active users, messages, tools, GPTs, Projects, apps, task types, repeat use, and available impact signals.
  • Interview teams around recurring friction, required sources, decisions, data boundaries, exception paths, and adoption burden.
  • Create a safe-use matrix and select three pilots using value, readiness, ownership, risk, reuse, and time-to-value.
31-60

Prove the pattern with bounded pilots

  • Build the smallest useful prompt, Project, custom GPT, connected-app, or automation pattern appropriate to each workflow.
  • Create evaluation sets for quality, completeness, groundedness, human authority, and exception handling.
  • Run weekly demos with live practice, reusable job aids, office hours, and champion coaching.
  • Instrument workflow and adoption measures before launch; document content, workflow, and outcome ownership.
61-90

Standardize winners and close the learning loop

  • Decide standardize, scale, repair, hold, or retire for each pilot.
  • Publish playbooks with purpose, inputs, prompt/workflow, data rules, human checkpoints, owner, measures, and review date.
  • Launch an adoption scorecard, champion network, escalation channel, and monthly capability review tied to new releases and real work.
  • Transfer workflow ownership to functions while retaining central governance and portfolio visibility.

Three gates before scale

Work gate

The workflow is materially better, not merely more AI-intensive.

Trust gate

Sources, data, human authority, exceptions, ownership, and review are explicit.

Adoption gate

People repeat the pattern in real work and the outcome can be measured.

Deliverables by day 90

Workflow portfolio

Prioritized backlog with value, readiness, risk, owner, adoption burden, evidence standard, and current decision.

Governance spine

Safe-use matrix, GPT/workflow ownership, review cadence, human authority, escalation, and retirement rules.

Tested playbook library

Reusable patterns for prompts, Projects, GPTs, apps, and workflows - each attached to a real task and outcome.

Enablement cadence

Release radar, weekly demos, monthly deep dives, office hours, champion coaching, and capability updates.

Adoption scorecard

Usage context paired with workflow, quality, economics, risk, ownership, and learning-speed measures.

Learning return

Every pilot produces a reusable pattern, a governance lesson, or a clear reason to stop.

Risks to manage from day one

Vanity adoption: activity rises without changed work. Orphaned assets: prompts or GPTs lack owners. Unsafe convenience: users bypass boundaries. Demo decay: techniques are not reinforced. Automation before clarity: a poor process is accelerated.
