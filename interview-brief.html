VOLTO CONSULTING · ROLE OPERATING BRIEF

Turn new AI capability into work that sticks.

Candidate thesis for the ChatGPT Power User Enablement Analyst role by Russell Dudek.

Actual work

Convert fragmented ChatGPT experimentation and rapid product change into a governed portfolio of repeatable workflows that employees adopt and leaders can measure.

Company moment

VOLTO publicly positions itself at the intersection of technology, talent, strategy, corporate training, AI and automation, and advisory-to-delivery outcomes. This role can make that promise tangible in daily knowledge work.

Candidate thesis

Do not optimize for message volume. Optimize the conversion rate from capability to governed work: fit, safe use, repeatability, adoption, and measurable outcome.

Operating tensions

  • Feature velocity vs. stable practice
  • Experimentation vs. data boundaries
  • Usage volume vs. business value
  • Shared standards vs. functional fit
  • Demos vs. durable behavior

Capability Transformer

Inputs: new capabilities, workflow friction, data boundaries.
Conversion: fit, safety, human authority, repeatability.
Outputs: adopted workflow, measured value, learning return.

Stakeholder system

Decision architecture

Prioritize

Which workflow has enough value, readiness, ownership, and adoption capacity to test now?

Bound

What data, source, action, and human-authority rules must remain explicit?

Prove

What quality, workflow, adoption, economics, and trust measures determine success?

Decide

Standardize, scale, repair, hold, or retire - with an owner and review date.

Questions Russell would use to understand the work

  1. What would make this role unquestionably successful six months from now?
  2. Is the primary customer VOLTO’s workforce, a client workspace, or both?
  3. Which functions already contain credible power users, and where is adoption ready to accelerate?
  4. Who owns workspace administration, security, privacy, legal interpretation, and GPT or app approval?
  5. Which recurring workflows offer the strongest combination of value, readiness, and reuse?
  6. How does VOLTO distinguish a popular use case from a valuable one?
  7. What usage, task, impact, and business-system data can this role access?
  8. How should new ChatGPT capabilities enter the monthly learning and governance cycle?
  9. How are successful playbooks maintained, improved, and transferred across teams?
  10. Which enablement format has produced the strongest repeat use so far?

How Russell would contribute

Build role-specific workflow patterns, teach teams how to use them in real work, establish visible human authority and governance, and connect adoption to measurable workflow outcomes. The contribution is hands-on, cross-functional, and designed to compound through reusable playbooks and a continuous learning cycle.
