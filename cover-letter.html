VOLTO Consulting

ChatGPT enablement fails when demos move faster than operating practice. People may send more messages, yet still lack reliable workflows, safe data boundaries, human checkpoints, reusable prompts, and measures tied to real work. VOLTO’s role description correctly brings those pieces together: workflow optimization, ongoing demonstrations, adoption analytics, prompt governance, and functional playbooks.

I bring the combination required to make that system practical. Through DudeWorth, I design AI-readiness and agentic workflow patterns around structured context, retrieval, human judgment, escalation, evaluation, and governance. At Vape-Jet, I work directly across production, support, quality, customer success, ERP, Jira, HubSpot, Slack, telemetry, and AI-assisted workflows - connecting signals to prioritized work, standard work, training, and accountability. Earlier, I built operating mechanisms in Amazon’s 24/7 customer-backward environment supporting approximately five million second-day orders annually.

My candidate vision for this role is a Capability Transformer: new ChatGPT functionality enters as potential, then passes through workflow fit, data and authority controls, repeatability, adoption, and measurement before it earns scale. The point is not “more AI.” The point is a shorter path from a feature release to a governed workflow that produces better work.

I would begin by baselining how teams use ChatGPT today, identifying three high-value workflows, mapping their data boundaries and decision owners, and pairing pilots with evaluation criteria and adoption instrumentation. Demonstrations would be attached to practice, office hours, and a champion network. Successful patterns would become owned playbooks; weak ones would be repaired or retired.

What I would deliver firstA baseline of current usage and workflow friction; three bounded pilots with evaluation and governance criteria; and a repeatable demo-to-adoption cadence that turns successful patterns into owned playbooks.

I prepared an independent candidate vision and working artifacts for this application. The role-specific candidate vision is available at https://russelldudek.github.io/Volto/; it includes the Capability Transformer, interview thesis, entry plan, and Power User Enablement Kit. I would welcome a conversation about whether this analyst serves VOLTO internally, within a client environment, or across both - because that distinction changes the governance model, stakeholder map, and measures of success.
