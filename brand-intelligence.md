## Visible company identity
The campaign uses a clearly typeset `VOLTO CONSULTING` identity above the fold, paired with `Candidate vision by Russell Dudek`. A sufficiently crisp, uncropped official logo asset could not be retrieved through the available official/public source path during this build. The public LinkedIn image-search result was cropped and unusable. No approximation, traced mark, redrawn mark, or third-party substitute is included.

## Color provenance
- `#303267` - source-sampled approximation of the public VOLTO dark navy.
- `#1487A4` and `#58BCD3` - source-sampled approximations of the public blue/teal identity.
- Violet, electric blue, and teal used inside the Capability Transformer are candidate-original role-metaphor accents.
- White, mist, and ink neutrals are candidate implementation colors.

## Typography
The public site presents a modern geometric-sans character, but no authoritative font token could be verified. The implementation uses a broadly available sans-serif stack and reproduces the public character through hierarchy, weight, tracking, and density. No font binaries are committed and no substitute is represented as VOLTO's official typeface.

## Visual grammar
VOLTO's public identity uses clean technology-consulting geometry, high-contrast navy/blue, broad service language, and an outcomes-oriented message. The campaign translates that grammar into a restrained editorial system and a role-specific Capability Transformer. It does not copy an internal interface or imply endorsement.

## Asset package
`assets/brand/README.md` records the logo constraint. No recreated or third-party logo is used.

## Direct visual and motion corrections
The initial flat SVG transformer was rejected because it looked diagrammatic and amateur. A first WebGL correction improved runtime behavior but remained wireframe-first and prototype-like. A subsequent volumetric correction improved craft but stopped after its introductory sequence and allowed semantic hero components to crowd one another at laptop width.

The current execution uses a full-viewport volumetric glass funnel with controlled mesh, luminous gradient rim, grounded multi-ring plinth, integrated input and outcome cards, and a central human-authority orb. Desktop motion has two role-specific layers: an opening causal transformation and a quiet continuous operating state. Forward packets represent continuing capability intake and workflow conversion; the slower return path represents reusable learning entering the next enablement cycle. The animation pauses when the page is hidden and stops entirely under reduced motion. Touch and narrow-screen devices receive a separately composed semantic funnel rather than compressed desktop WebGL. Desktop and laptop coordinates were rebalanced to preserve positive gaps between copy, input cards, center scene, output cards, and the summary bar.
